# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** [Discussions](https://github.com/haydenk/emend/discussions).
- **Something is broken:** a [bug report](https://github.com/haydenk/emend/issues/new/choose).
  A minimal page or configuration that shows the problem helps most.
- **Security problems:** privately, as the [security policy](.github/SECURITY.md) describes.

The [documentation](docs/README.md) covers installing, configuring and
customizing the theme. For questions about Hugo itself, the
[Hugo forum](https://discourse.gohugo.io) is the best place.
